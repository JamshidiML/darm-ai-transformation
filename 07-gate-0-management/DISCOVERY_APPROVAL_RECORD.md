## 1. Decision requested

Approve a ten-working-day discovery sprint to validate the feasibility, value, risk, ownership, and exact scope of a future Roll Quality Intelligence pilot.

This approval is **not** approval for:

- a company-wide AI platform;
- a 12-week pilot budget;
- machine control;
- ERP writeback;
- automatic product release;
- automatic customer communication;
- employee-performance monitoring;
- use of confidential data in unapproved public AI services.

## 2. Discovery scope

The discovery team is authorized to assess approved representative samples and metadata from:

- INTEX / ERP;
- ELSIS / inspection system;
- QC and laboratory records;
- selected production/process records;
- rework, scrap, quality-hold, and release records;
- complaint evidence where required and approved;
- Finance/Controlling quality-cost information.

The team will use the minimum data necessary and follow IT/security, privacy, confidentiality, retention, and employee-participation requirements.

## 8. Success criteria for Gate 1

Discovery is considered complete when:

- critical sources and owners are documented;
- representative samples are assessed;
- linkage rates are measured, not assumed;
- data-quality limitations are visible;
- at least one meaningful baseline or an explicit measurement plan exists;
- one narrow pilot scope is recommended;
- security, privacy, and IP requirements are defined;
- cost, effort, value, and risks are transparent;
- management can make a clear Go, Revise, or Stop decision.
