# Mohsen Jamshidi — Internal AI Leadership Strategy

**Classification:** Personal working document. Do not include in the corporate management package.

## 1. Positioning

The objective is not to become known as “the employee who uses ChatGPT.” The objective is to become the trusted internal leader who converts AI into measurable industrial value while protecting operations, know-how, quality, employees, and company strategy.

Recommended positioning statement:

> I am building a low-risk, measurable AI transformation path for Darmstädter and Klevers, beginning with quality, traceability, and technical knowledge, then scaling toward industrial and materials intelligence only after value is proven.

## 2. Leadership principles

- Lead with business and industrial problems, not tools.
- Start with real company data and bounded decisions.
- Protect confidentiality and avoid shadow AI.
- Build credibility with Production and Quality before promoting advanced models.
- Treat operators and experts as co-designers and knowledge owners.
- Be transparent about uncertainty, limitations, and failed hypotheses.
- Document every decision, result, benefit, and lesson.
- Build internal capability rather than permanent external dependence.
- Keep SmartCoat, personal startup ambitions, and company-owned work clearly separated unless formally approved.

## 3. First 90-day leadership goals

1. Obtain approval for the two-week discovery sprint.
2. Secure one executive sponsor.
3. Build a cross-functional team with R&D, Production, Quality, Packaging, IT, and Finance.
4. Complete the source inventory and ownership map.
5. Prove or disprove roll-level linkage using real samples.
6. Establish a Finance-approved quality-cost baseline.
7. Select one line and product family.
8. Present an evidence-based 12-week pilot proposal.
9. Create a monthly one-page management update.
10. Establish a professional record of decisions, risks, architecture, and value.

## 5. Career milestones

| Evidence created | Credible role progression |
|---|---|
| Discovery approved and delivered | AI Transformation Lead for Discovery |
| Pilot approved and governed | AI Transformation Lead / Industrial AI Project Lead |
| Pilot creates validated operational value | Industrial AI Program Lead |

| AI contributes to patents, funded R&D, customer innovation, and scalable capability | Internal entrepreneur / Head of Industrial AI / future CAIO-track role depending on company structure |

Titles are outcomes of trusted responsibility and results, not the initial objective.

## 7. Evidence portfolio

Maintain a private, permission-safe record of:

- approved charters;
- sanitized architecture diagrams;
- KPI definitions;
- data-quality methods;
- synthetic/demo prototypes;
- funding applications;
- presentation materials;
- management decisions;
- validated benefits;
- lessons learned;
- publications or patents approved by the company.

Never use confidential company data, formulations, customer names, or internal results externally without explicit authorization.

## 8. Monthly leadership report

One page:

- objective;
- progress;
- measurable findings;
- business value by confidence level;
- risks/blockers;
- decisions required;
- next 30 days;
- budget/status.

## 9. Personal boundaries

- Do not overpromise autonomous AI.
- Do not position colleagues as obstacles.
- Do not present unvalidated hypotheses as company facts.
- Do not bypass IT, Quality, management, or employee processes.
- Do not mix company IP with personal repositories or startup assets.
- Do not let the project become a collection of presentations without operational evidence.

## 10. Long-term ambition

Build the capability to lead a secure industrial intelligence program that combines technical-textile expertise, materials science, R&D, quality, production, computer vision, data engineering, knowledge systems, and AI.

The strongest long-term position is not “AI specialist” alone. It is:

> Industrial Materials and AI Transformation Leader with real domain ownership, measurable operational results, and the ability to connect science, production, data, people, and strategy.
